Synthetic chapter, Section 2 — An ideal spring
An ideal spring stores elastic potential energy U = (1/2) k x². Here k > 0 is the spring constant and x is the signed displacement from equilibrium. This model applies within the spring's elastic range. U depends on x², so equal positive and negative displacements store equal energy. At fixed k, doubling the displacement magnitude multiplies stored energy by four. This section does not discuss real spring failure, heat loss, or oscillation periods.
