# Genre constraints

Use only the row relevant to the artifact. Required templates and supplied house style take precedence over ordinary preferences.

| Genre | Preserve | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Academic/scientific | Defined terms, methods, claim strength, source scope, useful passive voice and field-appropriate signposting | Unsupported significance, casualization, removing every hedge or nominalization |
| Technical/specification | Stable labels, repeated exact terms, parallel procedures, necessary qualifications | Synonym variation that changes an interface or concept; rhetorical flourish in instructions |
| Legal/policy | Modality, defined terms, exceptions, scope, deliberate repetition and template language | Simplifying away obligations or permissions; claiming legal validation from a prose edit |
| Application/personal statement | Supplied experiences, individual diction and proportionate reflection | Invented adversity, sensory memories, emotions, revelations or grand future promises |
| Marketing/advocacy | Requested energy, audience, defensible benefits and evaluation | Invented proof, absolute claims without grounds, deleting all persuasive language merely to sound neutral |

For sparse application notes, build a coherent account from the facts present. Do not infer a childhood ambition or a life-changing lesson just because that would make a conventional essay arc. If the notes cannot support the requested passage, mark the specific gap outside the clean draft or use an explicitly requested placeholder.
